# Content and positioning

Positioning: mobile and web developer building digital products with attention to accessibility, design and engineering. Lead with mobile expertise; demonstrate broader capabilities through verified case studies.

Voice: first person, direct, specific and natural. No inflated claims, generic AI marketing copy, decorative emojis or arbitrary skill percentages. Write Spanish and English as natural equivalents, not literal translations. Label work in progress and demos.

Home: identity, concise positioning, selected work and clear links to projects/CV/contact. About: owner-approved professional background and skills backed by evidence. Lab: real interactive experiments, not filler. Contact: verified links only. Never publish personal contact data or a CV without approval.

Draft hero copy, subject to approval: “Construyo productos digitales.” / “I build digital products.” No implied employment history, seniority or client relationships without confirmation.
